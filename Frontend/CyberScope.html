ABOUT CYBERSCOPE

Turning isolated incidents into connected intelligence.

Built for connected cyber-fraud investigation.

CyberScope is designed around the idea that individual fraud complaints can reveal much more when their shared entities and infrastructure are viewed together.

This frontend prototype establishes the investigation interface. Backend data pipelines, databases, intelligence models and automated detection systems can be connected later.

CASES Centralized investigation records and case monitoring.
ENTITIES Connections between phones, accounts, domains and IDs.
NETWORKS Visual representation of connected fraud infrastructure.
INTELLIGENCE A foundation for future automated risk analysis.
